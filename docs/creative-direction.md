# Creative direction: from the chain to possibility

## Brand premise

ChainBreaker Labs exists to help people choose intentionally. The website
connects that purpose to a real first product, Infinyte, without inventing
traction, testimonials, released products, contact addresses, or store links.

The supplied banner suggested a laboratory, experimentation, and broken chains.
The new vector symbol retains those ideas in a simpler flask/broken-link mark.
The original banner is an input reference, not a production asset.

## Visual language

Warm ivory and charcoal keep the experience human and editorial. Acid lime
marks moments of possibility. Chrome represents the weight of old constraints.
Manrope provides clarity; Instrument Serif creates a more expressive voice for
the manifesto and the final invitation. Typography and photographs lead the
composition instead of generic feature tiles or decorative interface panels.

## Motion story

1. **Recognize:** an intact chain and a question about automatic living.
2. **Question:** the same central link breaks; a thin lime filament appears.
3. **Choose:** the two sides separate and create room for a new chapter.

Three consistent AI-generated photographic keyframes crossfade as scroll
progress changes, with modest scale/rotation and a deterministic 24-particle
field. This is a layered keyframe experience, not generated continuous video or
a claim of a full 3D physical simulation. There is no scroll hijacking. Pointer
response is limited to devices with a fine pointer.

The website is silent. The original sound control and all Web Audio ownership
were removed at the owner's request.

With reduced motion, the story becomes three readable articles and a static
illustration. Without JavaScript, Spanish content and product links remain
available. The brand and framed Dashboard are decorative; real product actions are
ordinary accessible links.

## Research sources

- [Barty-Bart: Opus 5.5 drone fly-through website prompts](https://github.com/Barty-Bart/opus-55-10k-websites):
  scroll-driven camera storytelling, consistent frames, and independent mobile pacing.
- [Yan Mantovani: a portfolio film built with code and an original soundtrack](https://www.yanmantovani.com/en/blog/filme-de-portfolio-com-codigo-e-ia):
  deliberate scene direction and code-generated sound.
- [KitCut's attributed Claude animation collection](https://kitcut.ai/):
  examples of code-driven visuals and motion.

These sources attribute their work to Opus 5.5. Their authors' model attribution
is not an independent verification by this project. No third-party footage,
music, branding, or creative source code has been copied into this website.

## Resource ownership

Browser effects have explicit teardown: one scroll-frame request at a time,
released listeners and observers. Page restoration initializes fresh ownership and reconciles menu DOM
state. Generated assets and fonts are local, with no external asset request
during an ordinary visit. Images use WebP and noncritical images are lazy-loaded.
